اللَّهُ جَلَّ جَلَالُهُ

Knowing Allah (ﷻ)

هُوَ اللَّهُ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ عَالِمُ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ ۖ هُوَ الرَّحْمَٰنُ الرَّحِيمُ

"He is Allah, other than whom there is no deity, Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He is the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful." — Surah Al-Hashr (59:22)

True wisdom begins with Tawheed—recognizing the Oneness, Mercy, Majesty, and Perfection of our Creator, relying upon Him in every step of life.

مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ﷺ

His Beloved Messenger (ﷺ)

وَمَا أَرْسَلْنَاكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةً لِّلْعَالَمِينَ

"And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds." — Surah Al-Anbiya (21:107)

Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) is the ultimate guide of character, compassion, truthfulness, and living Sunnah for all humanity.

الْقُرْآنُ الْكَرِيمُ

The Noble Quran

إِنَّ هَٰذَا الْقُرْآنَ يَهْدِي لِلَّتِي هِيَ أَقْوَمُ

"Indeed, this Quran guides to that which is most suitable and gives good tidings to the believers..." — Surah Al-Isra (17:9)

The eternal, preserved Word of Allah—a healing for hearts, light for the soul, and clear guidance from darkness into light.

Virtue of Learning Quran

خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ

"The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it."
— Sahih al-Bukhari (5027)
Reward of Recitation

مَنْ قَرَأَ حَرْفًا مِنْ كِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَلَهُ بِهِ حَسَنَةٌ، وَالْحَسَنَةُ بِعَشْرِ أَمْثَالِهَا

"Whoever recites a single letter from the Book of Allah will receive one good deed, and each good deed is multiplied by ten."
— Jami` at-Tirmidhi (2910)
Seekers of Knowledge

مَنْ سَلَكَ طَرِيقًا يَلْتَمِسُ فِيهِ عِلْمًا سَهَّلَ اللَّهُ لَهُ بِهِ طَرِيقًا إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ

"Whoever travels a path in search of knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise."
— Sahih Muslim (2699)
Prophetic Character

إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ مَكَارِمَ الْأَخْلَاقِ

"I was only sent to perfect noble character."
— Al-Adab Al-Mufrad (273)
Send Blessings Upon the Beloved Prophet (ﷺ)

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ

"O Allah, send prayers upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent prayers upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim; indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious."
